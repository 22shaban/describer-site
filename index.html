Turn any image into text

Describe screenshots, photos and diagrams in detail, then paste the text into ChatGPT or any AI instead of attaching the image.

Drop files anywhere on this page, or paste with Ctrl+V. PNG, JPG, WEBP, HEIC, PDF and short videos work.

Getting started takes about a minute
  1. Get a free key at aistudio.google.com. Sign in with Google, then choose Create API key.
  2. Paste it below and click Test key.
  3. Add an image, a screenshot or a PDF, then click Describe images.

More options

Your keys and images go straight from your browser to the AI services you use. Free tiers may use your inputs to improve their products, so don't send private images. Measured colors are taken from the pixels of your image and given to the AI as a reference. If a model fails (limit reached or not found), the next one is tried automatically.

Requests sent today from this browser: none

Backup AI services (used automatically if Google's limit is reached)

Order of use: Gemini models first, then Groq, then OpenRouter. Backup models are usually less detailed than Gemini, and the images you send go to whichever provider answers. Groq's list does not say which models can read images, so the tool skips models that refuse them. Free limits and model names change often, so use the Find buttons.

History

Saved only in this browser. Describing the same image again with the same settings reuses the saved text and uses no quota.

Check an image for AI signals

Stays on your device

Reads what is written inside the file: Content Credentials (C2PA), the "digital source type" label, the software or AI tool named, image-generator settings, camera data and location. It names the AI only when the file itself says so. You can also drop images anywhere on this page.

Shrink and convert images

Stays on your device

Makes images smaller so they fit an upload limit. Re-saving a picture does not keep the original's embedded information, such as camera data, location and Content Credentials. If you need those, keep the original. You can also drop images anywhere on this page.

Combine images into one PDF

Stays on your device

One PDF counts as one upload. It uses the images already in the Describe tab, in their current order, so reorder them there first with the arrows. Images you drop on this page are added to that list.

Privacy and saved data
  • The extra tools (AI-signal check, shrink, PDF) run on your computer, and the files you give them are not uploaded.
  • This page is a set of static files. There is no server of its own, so the people who host it never see your images, text or keys.
  • Your images and requests go straight from your browser to the AI service you choose: Google Gemini, and Groq or OpenRouter if you add them. Their terms apply, and free tiers may use inputs to improve their products.
  • Other sites are contacted only when needed: Google Fonts for the typeface, and jsDelivr for the OCR, PDF reading and diagram preview libraries.
  • Saved in this browser only: your keys if you tick "Remember my keys" (stored as plain text, so only use that on a computer you trust), your history of descriptions with tiny thumbnails (up to 40), presets, your last-used settings, today's request counts, and theme choices.
